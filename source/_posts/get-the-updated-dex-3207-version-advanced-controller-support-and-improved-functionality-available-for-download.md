**New Controller:** Added support for the [Hercules DJ Control GLOW](https://support.hercules.com/en/product/djcontrolglow-en/); An illuminated addition to the DEX family, ready to make your performance stand out.

**Four-Deck Stability:** Encountered crashes with some 4-deck controllers? Those are a thing of the past.

**[TIDAL](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) Tweaks:** We’ve smoothed out the experience by ensuring you can now load more than 50 playlists seamlessly. However, **note that the AIUnmixEQ™ will no longer be accessible for TIDAL tracks**. This restriction is now a necessary requirement. 

**Sprinkling of Improvements:** Because every DJ deserves a little extra, we’ve fine-tuned some minor elements to elevate your experience.

## DEX 3.20.6 Recap:

  
**KJs Rejoice:** No more song/singer duplicates in your history. Plus, swiftly find all songs by your favorite singer with a simple search query. And, we took care of that pesky bug that didn’t let you favorite the first track in the catalog/search results.

**Controller Compatibility Boost:** Enhanced mappings for controllers including Pioneer DDJ-1000, Pioneer DDJ-1000 SRT, Numark DJ2GO2 Touch, and Numark MixTrack Platinum FX.

**Cleaner Browser Experience:** Third-party streaming services will now be collapsed by default.

**Beatport/Beatsource Bliss:** Introducing guest mode improvements and dedicated icons to spruce up your interface.

**Advanced Audio Configurations:** The setAudioDevice() function now comes with (optional) advanced audio routing settings. This is for the tech-savvy DJs looking for an edge.

As always, version 3.20.7 is a free update for existing DEX 3 customers. Because your commitment to us deserves our continued dedication to improving your DJ software experience.
