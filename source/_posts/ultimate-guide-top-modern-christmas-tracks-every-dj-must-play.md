## Ultimate Guide: Top Modern Christmas Tracks Every DJ Must Play

[![](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/djchristmasplaylist-coverimage.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/djchristmasplaylist-coverimage.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "DJ Christmas Playlist Guy")

Around PCDJ HQ we’ve already begun to [DEX THE HALLS](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/). With dreams of a robust, red-clad jolly, cookie gobbling man on the way down a chimney nearby soon — it’s time for **Christmas DJ Playlist** sharing!

![DJ playlist reindeer](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/merryxmas-pcdj-deer-no-card.jpg?fit=239%2C300&ssl=1 "PCDJ chritmas DJ playlists")

Let’s admit it. Christmas parties are **typically our most predictable and boring DJ gigs** yearly. The corresponding **christmas music** we’re expected to DJ with is often dated and low-energy. There’s nothing new here, just drink your hearty mug of nogg and carefully devise your plan to be under the mistletoe with Jane from accounting before nights-end (the reindeer antler headband she’s wearing is just icing on the gingerbread man).

In polling a handful of our **mobile DJ software** users, each of whom are ‘lucky’ enough to have multiple Christmas parties on this years docket, we were given a few different **Christmas DJ playlists** they felt snubbed the status quo — providing a more musically unique atmosphere. If not for the party-goers, do it for yourself and your own piece of fruit-cake filled mind. There’s only so many bells to be jingled!

This **Christmas DJ playlist** comes from Monica Wright of About Class DJ Service in Reno NV. What do you think of her playlist? Have music to add or a **Christmas playlist** of your own? Feel free to include it in the comments section below!
